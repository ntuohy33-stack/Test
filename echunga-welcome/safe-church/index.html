About

Safe Church

Every person is created in the image of God, and every person deserves to be safe, respected and cared for.

Church should be a place where children, young people and adults can worship, grow and belong without fear. We take that seriously, and we want anyone with a concern to know exactly who to speak to.

As a congregation of the Uniting Church in South Australia, we are part of its Safe Church program, which helps churches provide a safe physical, emotional and spiritual environment for everyone.

If you have a concern

If something isn’t right, please speak up. These are the people to contact.

Someone is in immediate danger
Call SA Police on 000.
A child may be at risk of harm
Child Abuse Report Line (CARL): 13 14 78.
A concern about someone in a church leadership or responsible role
Safe Church Committee, Uniting Church SA: (08) 8236 4248.
You can also speak to someone at Echunga
Sarah Mosbey: families@echunga.ucasa.org.au.
If your concern is about Sarah or any other church leader, please contact the Safe Church Committee directly.
