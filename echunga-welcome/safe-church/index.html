About

Safe Church

Every person is created in the image of God, and every person deserves to be safe, respected and cared for.

Church should be a place where children, young people and adults can worship, grow and belong without fear. We take that seriously, and we want anyone with a concern to know exactly who to speak to.

As a congregation of the Uniting Church in South Australia, we are part of its Safe Church program, which helps churches provide a safe physical, emotional and spiritual environment for everyone.
