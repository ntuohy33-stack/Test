Read & Reflect

Gentle & Lowly

Dane Ortlund

A searching and tender exploration of the heart of Jesus. One of the most pastoral books written in recent years.

Making Sense of God

Timothy Keller

For anyone asking hard questions about faith in a secular age. Intelligent, accessible, and fair.

The Emotionally Healthy Leader

Peter Scazzero

For those in leadership — church, workplace, or family. Challenges the way we lead from the inside out.

One Thousand Gifts

Ann Voskamp

A lyrical, honest meditation on gratitude and the practice of noticing grace in ordinary life.

The Meaning of Marriage

Timothy & Kathy Keller

Theologically rich and practically wise. Recommended for couples at any stage.

Being the Bad Guys

Stephen McAlpine

A clear-eyed and encouraging guide to living as a Christian in a culture that has moved on from faith. Honest and hopeful.
