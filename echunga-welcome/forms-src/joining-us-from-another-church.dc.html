Two things worth knowing

We do not baptise anyone a second time; whatever church it happened in, your baptism stands. If you have a conviction that you would like to be baptised anyway, our minister would be happy to meet with you to talk this through.

And if you were baptised as an adult believer in a Baptist or similar church, you are already regarded as a confirmed member of that church — you will be welcomed here by transfer and will not be asked to be confirmed again.

What happens next

  1. 1A conversation, so we know each other a little.
  2. 2If a transfer is needed, we write to your previous church. Sometimes that takes a few weeks.
  3. 3Church Council receives you, and we welcome you in a Sunday service.
