What you will be asked

On the day, in front of everybody, you will be asked to say four things. They are the same four for everyone, everywhere in our church:

  • That Jesus Christ is your Saviour and Lord.
  • That you mean to follow him in ordinary daily life.
  • That you intend to take a real part in the life of this church and support its work.
  • That you will seek God's just and good ways in the world.

Nobody is asked to be certain about everything, or to have their life sorted out.

What happens next

  1. 1We sit down over a cuppa and talk it through properly. No rush.
  2. 2Church Council is asked to approve. This is a warm formality, not an examination.
  3. 3We pick a Sunday that suits you and the people you want there.
  4. 4On the day you make your promises, you are signed with the cross, and hands are laid on you while we pray for you.
