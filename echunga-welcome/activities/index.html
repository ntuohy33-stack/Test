Activities

At Echunga Family Church, connection happens in all kinds of ways — over coffee, craft, and conversation, as well as through small groups and community events. There’s something here for everyone.

Craft, Cuppa & Cards

Weekly · 1pm–4pm$2 per week

A relaxed weekly gathering in Wandeen, Main Street, Echunga. Bring your knitting, sewing, quilting, colouring, or jigsaw — or simply come for the cards and conversation. Everyone is welcome, whether you come occasionally or every week.

A small plate of afternoon tea is appreciated but not essential.

Men’s Coffee

Third Thursday of each month · 9:30am

A relaxed monthly catch-up over coffee at Farm Gate Services, Echunga. All men welcome.
