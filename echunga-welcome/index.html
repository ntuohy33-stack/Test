About Echunga Family Church

We’re a warm, Christ-centred community in the heart of the Adelaide Hills.

For over a century, Echunga Family Church has been a place of worship, belonging, and service — welcoming people from all walks of life.

What to expect on a Sunday

Sunday mornings at Echunga are relaxed, genuine, and multigenerational. We gather for worship together at 10am.

Sunday Worship

10am main service

  1. You’re greeted at the doorNo need to know what to do.
  2. We worship togetherThe service includes singing, Scripture, a Bible message, and time for prayer.
