Our Story

Part of the Hills for generations

Echunga Family Church has been part of the Adelaide Hills community for generations. We are an evangelical congregation within the Uniting Church in Australia — holding to orthodox Christian faith, committed to the authority of Scripture, and passionate about seeing people come to know Jesus.

The Uniting Church was formed on 22 June 1977, bringing together Congregational, Methodist, and Presbyterian traditions. We are proud to be part of that heritage while remaining focused on our core calling: making and growing disciples in this community.

We are a member congregation of Generate Presbytery — a network of evangelical churches across South Australia committed to gospel renewal, mission, and discipleship.

Vision & Mission

We believe Jesus came to bring fullness of life to all people. The local church is where that life is modelled, shared, and grown.

Our Vision

To see a new generation in the Hills find Jesus and His people.

Our Mission

Knowing Jesus, Go — Caring & Sharing.

Growing as followers of Jesus, we care for those around us and help others come to faith. Our hope is to see people encounter God’s love, grace, and the hope that only He brings.

Meet our Minister

Rev Nicholas Tuohy

Hi, I’m Nicholas — and I’m glad you’re here.

I’ve been married to Amanda for 33 years, and have been serving as a pastor for over 25 years across Australia and the UK — in churches ranging from 50 to 1500 people. Before ordination I spent a decade as a professional chef, which still shapes how I think about hospitality and community.

My own story isn’t a straight line. I grew up with a single Greek-Cypriot mother in a household that was generous, chaotic, and at times painful. By 19 I was playing in bands, experimenting with New Age spirituality, and working in kitchens. My encounter with Jesus came through the most unlikely of sources — a member of a Christian heavy metal band sharing the gospel at my local pub. It changed everything.

Since then I’ve pursued a deep commitment to biblically grounded, theologically serious, and pastorally warm ministry. I hold postgraduate qualifications in theology, ministry formation, and counselling.
