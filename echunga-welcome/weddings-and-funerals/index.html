Weddings

Congratulations on your decision to be married

Thank you for considering Echunga Family Church for your wedding ceremony. Marriage is a joyful and significant occasion, and we count it a privilege to host weddings in this beautiful church building. We hope to make the process as clear, warm and straightforward as possible as you plan your day.

Fees

Fees
Church hire$400
Minister$200
Verger / cleaner$100
Organist / pianist$80
Sound and presentation$200
Total full-service fee$980

If you arrange your own Christian minister, organist or pianist, those fees do not apply.

How booking works

  1. Contact the church office to check that the church is available, and to arrange a viewing of the building.
  2. Once your date and time are confirmed, a $100 deposit secures the booking.
  3. If you would like Rev Nicholas Tuohy to conduct your ceremony, let us know when you enquire. If another minister is conducting the service, the ceremony must be led by a Christian minister, who is asked to make contact with Rev Nicholas once the booking is confirmed.
  4. Once your booking is in place, the church office will arrange access to the church for flowers and other preparations, and confirm the date and time of your rehearsal.
  5. If you would like to use the church’s keyboard pianist, please let us know as early as possible. If you wish to provide your own organist or pianist, tell us so we can confirm they are familiar with the church instrument.
  6. If you would like the details of a local florist, the church office will be happy to help.
  7. If you are interested in having your wedding livestreamed, ask the church office for details of our current livestream provider.
  8. An email confirming the final balance will be sent to you seven weeks before your wedding. Full payment is required no later than four weeks before the wedding.
  9. Please let your guests know that nothing is to be thrown inside the church. Rose petals may be thrown outside, but confetti is not permitted.

Payment

The $100 deposit is payable when your booking is confirmed, and the balance no later than four weeks before the wedding. Pay by bank transfer using the details on our Contact page, with the reference Wedding followed by your wedding date, then let the church office know once payment has been made.

Funerals

Please accept our sincere sympathy

We understand that planning a funeral often comes in the midst of grief, shock and sorrow. As a church community, we prayerfully seek to walk beside families with gentleness, care and respect, and to help make arrangements as clear and simple as possible.

Where to start

Please email Rev Nicholas Tuohy. There is no need to have anything worked out.
